Summary: Text Editor is an offline-first mobile application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your personal data or document content. Your files never leave your device.

1. Core Philosophy & Overview

At Text Editor ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe that your notes, code, and documents are private by default. This application was engineered with a strict offline-first and zero-telemetry architecture. We do not require you to create an account, log in with an email address, or connect to any cloud server to use the application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identification: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Document & Code Contents: Any text, code, script, or file you create, edit, or open remains strictly confidential on your local device.
  • Analytics & Telemetry: We do not track keystrokes, usage duration, feature clicks, or session recordings.
  • Device Identifiers: We do not collect advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), IP addresses, IMEI numbers, or hardware serials.
  • Location Data: We do not request or track your GPS or network-based geographic position.

3. Local File Storage & Document Picker

Text Editor reads and writes files directly using your device's native file system and Android's Storage Access Framework (Document Picker).

  • When you use the Open action, you select the specific file via the native system picker. Text Editor only accesses that specific file for editing.
  • When you choose Save or Save As, the updated text is written directly back to the selected file path on your local storage.
  • Tabs and temporary unsaved session buffers are stored locally inside the app's sandboxed document directory and can be cleared at any time via Settings.

4. Device Permissions Explained

The app may request or use the following permissions on Android:

  • Storage Access (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required on supported Android versions to allow you to open existing files from your storage drives and save your edited files.
  • Haptic Feedback (VIBRATE): Used exclusively to deliver subtle tactile micro-vibrations when you interact with buttons and switches for improved user feedback.
  • Internet Access: Only utilized if you explicitly choose to open external links (such as viewing this Privacy Policy or Terms in your web browser). No editor data is ever sent over the network.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Text Editor contains no advertisements, no marketing SDKs, and no tracking libraries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Text Editor does not collect or transmit personal information of any kind, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks. It is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

7. Updates to this Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal compliance. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Since we do not collect your contact details, we encourage you to review this page periodically.
